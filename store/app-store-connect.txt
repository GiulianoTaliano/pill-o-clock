Notas (inglés, máx. 4000 bytes):

Pill O-Clock is a local-first medication reminder. There is no account or login: every feature is available right after onboarding. Medications, dose history, measurements and diary are stored only on the device (encrypted SQLite); we operate no backend.

To test a reminder: add a medication with a time 2 minutes ahead. A Time Sensitive notification with Take / Snooze / Skip actions fires and repeats every 5 minutes up to 2 times.

Permissions: camera is used only to scan medication barcodes (Argentina and US packages); photo library only to attach a picture of the medication box; location (optional) only to pin appointment places; Face ID only for the optional app lock.

The app does not diagnose, calculate doses or give medical advice. Allergy and duplicate-therapy notices are informational and tell users to consult their doctor or pharmacist. Crash diagnostics (Sentry) contain no health data. The iOS version shows no ads.
